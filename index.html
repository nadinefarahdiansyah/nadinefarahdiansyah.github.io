About

Designing with empathy,
building with purpose.

I’m a 2023 cohort student in the Department of Information Systems at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS), majoring in Digital Innovation. I’m drawn to the place where business, technology and design meet — turning ideas into digital products that actually create value.

My favorite proof is Trashney, a recycling app I researched, designed and tested over two iterations until it reached an Excellent usability score. Alongside UX, I lead media design for student organizations — most notably as Head of Information Media at ITS Astronomy Club, where I also created the club’s mascots.

I’m known for being adaptable, a quick learner and meticulous with details. Right now I’m expanding into data and AI through GCI World 2026 at the University of Tokyo’s Matsuo-Iwasawa Lab.

Turning the intention to recycle into real action.

A waste-management app with scheduled pickups, drop points and rewards. I ran the full design-thinking cycle — interviews, personas, competitive audit, wireframes and two rounds of Maze testing — cutting Pick Up misclicks from 73.5% to 18.7%.
